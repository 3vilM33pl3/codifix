# Drain evidence limits

The observed disconnect is not evidence that remote model execution was rolled
back, canceled or unbilled. D12 governs responder admission and lifetime, not
provider transactionality. The fake-responder success/cancel behavior is a test
of the proposed lifecycle, not proof of original remote outcomes.

There is no full request denominator, revenue effect, provider reconciliation
or measured production drain success rate. Endpoint removal alone does not prove
zero active work; a live process alone does not prove readiness. Report those
states separately and retain accepted-but-unconfirmed requests as uncertain.
