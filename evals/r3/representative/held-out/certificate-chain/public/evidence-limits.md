# Chain evidence and limits

The selected observations support a missing-intermediate presentation failure
for the clean client under T9. Valid dates alone do not supply an absent issuer;
a warm client's cached issuer explains why the symptom can differ by client.

No fleet-wide request denominator, customer outage duration, key-compromise
evidence or revocation-service result is supplied. Successful signature/name/time
checks in this fixture are not a full security certification. There is no proof
that every client uses the stated cache/fetch policy outside this experiment.

Report the directly observed failure and proposed repair separately from those
unknowns. Do not recommend suppressing certificate checks to obtain a green
health probe. A connection failing before its application request is not an
application-handler rejection.
