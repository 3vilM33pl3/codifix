# Trace evidence limits

The selected export shows diagnostic identity contamination and merged attempt
spans. It does not establish business-payload disclosure or duplicate completed
business effects. j2 explicitly demonstrates why absent sampled telemetry is not
proof of non-execution. Queue receipts and tracing have different purposes.

There is no complete trace population, clock alignment or incident loss count.
Do not infer complete latency, work-loss rates or a data breach from these spans.
Correct propagation repairs attribution under U20, not all observability or
business-delivery guarantees.
