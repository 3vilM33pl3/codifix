# Distribution evidence limits

An origin/version identifier shows where a component came from; it does not
fulfill D22's separate distribution metadata obligations. Text deduplication
does not merge ownership or class requirements. Installed and distributed sets
are intentionally different.

These invented classes do not establish compliance with any real license,
infringement, penalties or customer exposure. The broken output is staged, not
evidence it was distributed. Real license text, legal review, distribution logs
and recipient inventory are absent. Report the internal policy failure without
inventing those conclusions or publishing a repair.
