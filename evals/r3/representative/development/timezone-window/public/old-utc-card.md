# Retired UTC simplification card

An early proposal converted 01:30 once and repeated the resulting UTC time every
24 hours. It also used generated job IDs to distinguish executions. That proposal
predates revision 5's civil-date identity and gap/fold policy and is not current.

Running the host in UTC is still appropriate operationally, but does not replace
the customer zone or resolve repeated/missing civil times. The old card remains
in the handover archive so previous behavior can be explained.
