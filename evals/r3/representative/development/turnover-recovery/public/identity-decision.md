# Writer identity retirement — approved change 27

Approved 2026-08-23 by the company space administrator.

The departing maintainer's service credential and writer generation 8 are
retired. The gateway's authorized writer generation for Alder is now 9. An
instance restored from an older backup must not resume using generation 8 or
the retired credential. The credential value is deliberately absent here.

An administrator must enroll a replacement service identity and explicitly
grant its project/space capabilities. The replacement does not inherit authority
from the former maintainer's authorship or local role. Operators must verify the
new enrollment against the gateway before enabling publication.

Previously accepted knowledge retains its recorded author and provenance.
Credential retirement prevents future authorized transfers; it does not erase
already delivered copies or establish that earlier knowledge was false. Local
access policy and any recorded content withdrawal still apply to those copies.
