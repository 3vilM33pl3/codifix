# Retired metering guide

The early dashboard added parent and child costs and treated missing usage as
zero. It also excluded preparation from the campaign cap and silently retried
timeouts. Revision 3 replaces those behaviors; the old guide is retained only
to explain historical numbers.

For K9, neither 190 alone nor 380 from parent-plus-children describes all settled
campaign costs. Unknown usage is uncertainty, not a measurement of zero. A
partial completed-task report must not be promoted to official campaign evidence.
