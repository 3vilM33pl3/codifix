# Elm support archive policy P9

This fictional policy is the exercise's contract, not legal advice. Support
attachment payloads normally expire 30 days after capture. Metadata receipts
remain for 180 days. Search eligibility ends at payload expiry even if physical
cleanup has not run. Deletion from active storage is distinct from expiry and
from removal of recipients' copies or backup bytes.

An active case hold suspends payload destruction for covered case IDs, not for
every row in the project. Held payloads leave ordinary search at normal expiry;
only the restricted case-review role may access them. Metadata records the hold
and storage outcome without copying attachment content into audit messages.

Only a recorded release by the case authority ends a hold. Closing a support
ticket or a client request saying "please delete" is not a hold release. After
release, an already expired payload becomes eligible for verified cleanup; it
does not become searchable again or receive a fresh 30-day lifetime.
