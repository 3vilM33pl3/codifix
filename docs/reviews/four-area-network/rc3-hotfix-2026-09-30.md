## Defect and bounded fix

Trust observed signed gateway `issued_at` timestamps up to tens of microseconds
ahead of its healthy NTP clock. The rc.2 verifier rejected every future instant,
which explains intermittent status and answer-delivery failures. Four of five
same-ID rc.2 reads returned the retained one-citation answer; one was
unconfirmed. The rc.3 fix in `ac08624e` tolerates at most one second of future
skew for signed status/expertise replies. Signature, nonce, maximum response
lifetime and expiry checks remain. Focused tests cover a small future offset and
reject a five-second offset. No schema, permission, collection or Pi/PDS/Atrium
pin changes were made. Source revision:
`d535b3edf2cbf6ba817760ef743a01a4a31d7b41`.
