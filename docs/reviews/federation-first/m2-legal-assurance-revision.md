# M2 revision — Legal counsel and assurance leadership

Status: **implemented and validated financial preparation**.
Date: **15 September 2026**. Owner: founder / finance. This revision adds the
legal and assurance functions requested after the employee AI revision.

The working planning choice is **nine employees plus fractional legal counsel**.
The original eight roles remain; an additional Head of Security & AI Governance
starts in month 3. Fractional counsel starts in month 1. These arrangements,
dates and amounts are editable planning hypotheses, not hiring or supplier
commitments. The user requested both functions; the specific employment choices
and cost defaults are the implementation recommendation.

The standards do not prescribe these job titles or require a full-time hire.
This staffing choice is a Codifix planning recommendation. Consider both internal
agentic engineering and customer-facing AI when defining the AI-management
scope. A federated connection does not automatically place every connected
enterprise, deployment or agent inside Codifix's assurance scope.

Use **SOC 2 Type II examination/report**, rather than “SOC II certification”.
The internal lead prepares and maintains controls and evidence; independent
certification bodies issue ISO certificates, and an independent CPA firm issues
the SOC report. See the [dated primary-source register](m2-model/legal-assurance-sources.md)
for ISO and AICPA references and the boundaries of each claim.

## M3 handoff and assurance preparation

1. Validate legal service scope, qualified jurisdictions, turnaround/capacity,
   exceptional fundraising or transaction work, and the accounting/payroll split.
2. Validate recruitment availability and pay for a lead with relevant programme
   experience, practical AI governance and enough authority to coordinate the
   business. Confirm the engineering effort needed to operate controls.
3. Agree the organisational/system scope with providers, including Codifix's
   Memory gateway, independently administered deployments, customer runtimes,
   external providers and employee AI use.
4. Obtain separately scoped prices and timelines for ISO readiness support,
   independent internal audit, certification, surveillance/recertification, SOC
   2 Type II initial and recurring examinations, testing and evidence tools.
   Reconcile these with the historical allowance before presenting funding as
   covering the entire assurance programme. Unquoted extra scope is not zero.
5. Maintain the existing M3 requirements to measure employee AI use, engineering
   throughput, review/rework and customer-delivery capacity. More AI spending
   or an assurance hire does not prove the commercial targets achievable.

Planning sequence: M1 counsel and founder establish legal priorities; M3 lead
defines scope and control/evidence owners; before each pilot verify the actual
readiness and sharing gates; then operate controls and collect evidence over
the agreed period before seeking independent reports/certification. No report
or certification completion date is claimed by this financial model.
