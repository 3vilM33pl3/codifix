# Delivery economics and boundaries
Costs are driven by deployments, questions, attempts, tokens, collection size, replicas, transfer and human effort. They cover infrastructure, inference, deployment, identity/key operations, gateway/PDS, storage, transfer, recovery, security review, curation, administration and multi-party support. Failed and retried exchanges consume resources. Replication creates applicable cost without a new knowledge charge.
Regional salaries and employment-cost factors remain provisional. The approved eight-role hiring plan replaces the active historical schedule, which remains selectable for comparison. Delivery payroll and employee AI are each allocated once between cost of sales and operating expenses; contractors cover workload beyond reserved capacity. Gross margin is calculated from costs.
Codifix funds Memory service delivery by default. Customer agent-runtime spending is separate; its unmeasured rate remains PENDING. Assigning any of that cost to Codifix requires a numeric input. Every editable assumption has value, unit, source, date, owner and evidence status.
