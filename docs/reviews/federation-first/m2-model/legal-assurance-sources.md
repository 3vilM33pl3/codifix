# Legal counsel and assurance leadership: scope and sources

Research checked **15 September 2026**. Owner: founder / CEO. This note supplies
role definitions and public evidence for a financial revision. It does not select
hire dates, employment arrangements, salaries, retainers or audit budgets.
Published prices and salary ranges are reference points, not Codifix quotations.

## Proposed responsibilities

| Function | Proposed Codifix remit | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal counsel | Enterprise and pilot contracts; authorised knowledge-sharing agreements; IP and open-source licensing; privacy and data-processing terms; corporate governance and fundraising; employment matters; coordination of jurisdiction-specific advice. | Counsel advises on legal obligations and contracts. Engagement scope, qualified jurisdictions and specialist support require verification. |
| Head of Security & AI Governance | Own the information-security and AI-management systems; define scope; coordinate risks, policies, control owners, evidence, management reviews, remediation and customer assurance; manage the ISO and SOC examination programmes. | Engineering and other business owners operate their controls. Leadership retains accountability and resource decisions. The programme owner does not issue certificates or an independent SOC report. |
| Independent assurance providers | ISO certification, independent internal assurance where needed, and SOC 2 examination/reporting. | Scope, auditor independence, qualifications, fees and timetable require provider confirmation. Preparation and certification are distinct deliverables. |

These are proposed operating responsibilities, not requirements that the standards
prescribe a particular job title or a full-time employee. An internal owner needs
sufficient authority and time even where implementation support is outsourced.
Codifix should consider both employee agentic engineering and customer-facing AI
when defining its AI-management scope. This is a planning recommendation; the
final boundary needs explicit agreement with the relevant assurance providers.

## Standards and assurance terminology

| Reference | Verified public description | Implication for preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ISO/IEC 27001:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) | Requirements for an information security management system. The ISO page also lists the 2024 climate-action amendment. | Define the organisational and service scope, manage information-security risks, operate controls and retain evidence. |
| [ISO/IEC 42001:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | Requirements for an AI management system, applicable to organisations developing, providing or using AI systems. | Define AI responsibilities, risks, policies and the continuing management process; do not treat a model evaluation alone as management-system conformity. |
| [ISO certification](https://committee.iso.org/certification.html) | ISO does not perform certification or issue certificates. Certification bodies operate independently of ISO. | Budget certification separately from the internal programme and seek an appropriately accredited provider for the selected scope. |
| [AICPA SOC 2 report review checklist](https://assets.ctfassets.net/rb9cdnjh59cm/3xbcLlNc5rd72So4nQpNIk/7a3e8e5945c78c35fc5e116859b96e6a/SOC_2_Report_%C3%82_Review_Checklist.pdf) | Distinguishes a type 1 date from a type 2 period; identifies an independent service auditor; states that a licensed CPA firm issues the report; addresses tests of operating effectiveness. | Use **SOC 2 Type II examination/report**, not “SOC II certification”. Agree the covered system, trust-services categories and evidence period with the CPA firm. |

The AICPA checklist also calls for examining relevant systems, processes,
locations, third-party dependencies and customer responsibilities. For Codifix's
federated product, the proposed preparation work should therefore document the
boundaries between Codifix services, independently administered deployments,
customer agent runtimes and external providers. An assurance claim must describe
the actual scope; it must not imply every connected enterprise or agent is
covered automatically.

### Rejected pricing evidence

Search indexing for [Hythe Legal's fractional counsel
page](https://hythelegal.com/services/fractional-general-counsel) returned a
£5,000 monthly price and approximately 1.5 hours per working day. Reopening the
current primary page returned only the scope and engagement description, without
those figures. **Do not use the indexed price or hours as verified current cost
evidence.** The cause of the discrepancy was not established. The live page can
support a description of fractional counsel covering contracts, corporate and
privacy matters, but a fee requires a current quote.

## Financial-model and M3 handoff

- Record each selected amount, unit, date, source, owner and evidence status in
  the model. A value selected from a public range remains a Codifix planning
  hypothesis until hiring or engagement evidence supports it.
- Distinguish salaried headcount from externally purchased legal or assurance
  services. Apply employee AI and employee-linked overhead only to the selected
  employment treatment; do not automatically assign them to vendor personnel.
- Reconcile the new lines with existing legal, security-review and SOC budgets
  so overlapping scope is not counted twice.
- Obtain scoped quotations for initial certification/examination, recurring
  surveillance or reassessment, independent audit, testing and evidence tools.
  This research established no reliable all-in cost for the combined programme.
- Validate legal capacity, governance-lead availability, implementation effort,
  engineering participation and evidence-period requirements before promising
  certification or a SOC report by a particular date.

No supplier engagement, hiring commitment, procurement or certification claim is
created by this research.
