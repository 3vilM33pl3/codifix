## Narrative and evidence decisions

Codifix is the knowledge and governance network for the Agentic Enterprise. Independently governed business units own knowledge, agents and administration. Authorized edges exchange selected expertise. The first commercial proof is application/platform incident handover, using existing agent runtimes.

The first five slides explain the problem, the Engineering → OS → Enterprise analogy, the network mechanism and the bounded workflow without requiring ATProto knowledge. Omarchy is an analogy, not an affiliation or dependency. The workflow includes a cited answer, human action, recording a correction, review at origin and later eligible reuse. Local curation and renewed collection review are required; correction approval does not automatically rewrite ordinary local memory.

The deck distinguishes implementation, controlled demonstration, observed development results, hypotheses and future work. The R3 appendix reports 60 answers awaiting review and 0/15 complete coding sequences (one family, five conditions, three repeats), without claiming quality improvement. Private Q&A remains in Memory's addressed gateway mailbox, never the PDS or shared publication log. Gateway trust, retained readable copies and revocation limits are explicit. Native Spaces and broader delegated execution remain future work.

The Tom briefing was used as pre-meeting research about expertise, accountability, defensibility and capital discipline. The conversation has not happened. No feedback, endorsement, investor interest, customer traction, partner commitment, candidate pipeline, completed certification or validated ROI is implied.

Removed from the live slide content: the old task-success/perfect-recall/token-saving headlines, the causal pilot-failure story, blanket competitor negatives, decorative TAM/growth bubbles, legacy self-serve/operations forecast, preset margin assumptions and fixed monetary ask. Historical values survive only as clearly labelled audit history where relevant.
